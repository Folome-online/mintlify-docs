---
title: 'Claude.ai (Web)'
description: 'Bring OpenFinance into your Claude.ai chats as a custom connector. You sign in once with your OpenFinance account, so there is no API key to copy or store.'
---

Because the OpenFinance MCP server runs over Streamable HTTP, Claude.ai can connect to it directly, with nothing to install. The whole setup happens in Claude.ai's settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connectors settings">
    Open Claude.ai → **Settings** → **Connectors**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add custom connector">
    Click **Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    - **Name**: `OpenFinance`
    - **URL**: `https://api.openfinance.tech/agent/mcp`

    No header is needed. Claude.ai signs in to your OpenFinance account with OAuth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and approve">
    Click **Add**, then click **Connect** on the OpenFinance connector. You are sent to OpenFinance: sign in and click **Authorize** on the consent page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch it on">
    Open any chat and click the **tools menu**. OpenFinance should appear, toggle it on for the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Custom connectors are available on Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans. Skills installed via `npx skills add` only apply to local editors, not Claude.ai web.
</Note>
