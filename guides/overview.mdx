Each guide gives you the exact prompts to paste into your agent, tells you what a good answer looks like, and shows you how to confirm the result yourself. The reference pages explain each venue and tool on its own; the guides show how to put them together.

## How a guide works

Every guide follows the same shape:

1. **Goal.** What you will have done by the end.
2. **What you need.** Accounts, funds and connections to have ready.
3. **Steps.** Prompts to paste, in order, with what a good answer looks like. Steps that move money are marked **(moves money)**.
4. **Check it worked.** How to confirm the result yourself.
5. **Common mistakes.** What usually goes wrong and how to avoid it.

The prompts work in the [OpenFinance app chat](/product/chat) and in any AI client connected to the [MCP server](/mcp/overview), such as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Hermes or OpenClaw. If you have not connected an agent yet, start with [Quick install](/getting-started/quick-install) or the page for your client.

## Safe-agent basics

Keep these five habits in every guide:

- **Read first, trade second.** Ask for the balance, the price or the quote before you ask for the order.
- **Read the summary before you say yes.** Every trade needs your confirmation. Check the market, side, size and price.
- **Start small.** There is no practice mode, so your first trade on any venue should be one you would not mind losing.
- **Keep approvals on.** Do not put OpenFinance tools on an auto-approve list in your client.
- **Guard your key.** It acts as your whole account. Never paste it into a chat, a repository or a screenshot.

Nothing in these guides is financial advice. Trading carries a high risk of loss.
