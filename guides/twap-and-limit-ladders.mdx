By the end of this guide you will have worked a large order without hitting the book all at once: either as a TWAP that trades in slices over time, or as a ladder of limit orders spread across prices.

Here is why it matters. A single large market order eats through the order book and fills at worse and worse prices. The two methods avoid that in different ways:

| | TWAP | Limit ladder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spreads the order over | Time | Price |
| How it fills | Market slices, automatically, until the duration ends | Only when the price reaches each level |
| Where | Hyperliquid | Hyperliquid and Polymarket |
| Good for | Getting filled with less impact | Buying dips or selling into strength at chosen prices |

## What you need

- Funds on the venue: USDC on Hyperliquid, or pUSD in your Polymarket deposit wallet. See [Fund each venue](/guides/fund-each-venue).
- A total size, and either a duration (TWAP) or a price range and number of levels (ladder).

## Run a TWAP on Hyperliquid

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the book first">
    ```text
    Show the Hyperliquid order book and 24 hour volume for SOL perps.
    ```

    Compare your total size with what sits near the top of the book. That tells you whether a TWAP is worth it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the TWAP">
    ```text
    Buy 20 SOL perps with a TWAP over 60 minutes, with randomized timing. Show me the total size, duration, slice count and total value before placing it.
    ```

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it (moves money)">
    Reply yes. The TWAP now trades on its own until the duration ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the slices">
    ```text
    Show my TWAP fills so far: filled size, average price and what is left.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop it early if you need to (moves money)">
    ```text
    Stop my running SOL TWAP.
    ```

    Slices that already filled stay filled. Your position is whatever has executed so far.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Place a limit ladder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the ladder">
    ```text
    On Hyperliquid, place 5 limit buy orders for ETH perps, 0.02 ETH each, spaced evenly from 2% to 6% below the current mid price, good till cancelled. Show me every order with its price before placing.
    ```

    Several orders can go in one request, and you confirm them together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it (moves money)">
    Check every price and size, then reply yes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage the ladder">
    ```text
    Show my open ETH orders. Cancel any that have not filled in 24 hours.
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

On Polymarket the same idea works with a batch of limit orders:

```text
On that Polymarket market, place 4 limit buys for Yes at 0.38, 0.36, 0.34 and 0.32, 10 dollars each. Show the batch first.
```

Every order needs your confirmation, and nothing here is financial advice.

## Check it worked

- For a TWAP: the fills add up to the total size by the end, or to what filled before you stopped it.
- For a ladder: every level shows in your open orders at the price you approved.

## Common mistakes

- **Sizes that do not match the market's precision.** Hyperliquid rejects sizes with too many decimals. Let the agent round to the market's size step.
- **Prices off the tick.** On Polymarket each price must sit on the market's tick size.
- **Levels too far from the market.** Hyperliquid rejects prices too far from the current price. Keep the ladder within a sensible range.
- **Forgetting a ladder is live.** Resting orders can fill days later. Cancel what you no longer want.
- **Treating TWAP as a price guarantee.** Each slice fills at the market at that moment.
