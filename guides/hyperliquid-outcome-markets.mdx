By the end of this guide you will have read a Hyperliquid outcome market correctly, bought whole shares on the side you chose, and sold or held them through expiry.

These outcome markets are also called HIP-4. A share price between 0 and 1 reads like a probability. They settle in **USDC** from the same Hyperliquid balance you use for perps, and sizes are **whole shares**. See [Hyperliquid outcome markets](/markets/hyperliquid-outcomes).

## What you need

- USDC in your Hyperliquid account. No second token is needed. See [Fund each venue](/guides/fund-each-venue).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="List the markets in two groups">
    ```text
    List the outcome markets on Hyperliquid. Show standalone Yes or No markets and grouped questions in separate sections, with the current price of each side.
    ```

    A **standalone** market is one Yes or No statement. A **question** groups several named outcomes where only one can be true, such as a set of price ranges.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the real question, not the label">
    Recurring markets reuse the same display name. Ask for the meaning in words:

    ```text
    For that market, tell me in one sentence what Yes means: the underlying, the target price or range, and the exact expiry time in UTC.
    ```

    For a price question, ask which range each named outcome covers before you pick one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the book">
    ```text
    Show the order book for the Yes side of that market and the spread.
    ```

    A thin book means your price can move as you fill. Use a limit price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the order in whole shares">
    ```text
    Buy 20 Yes shares at no more than 0.42. Show me the order and the total cost in USDC first.
    ```

    If you ask in dollars, the agent rounds to whole shares. Very small orders can be rejected for being below the venue's minimum order value; raise the share count if that happens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it (moves money)">
    Reply yes if the side, share count and price match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the position">
    ```text
    What outcome shares do I hold, and what are they worth at the current price?
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell early or hold to expiry (moves money)">
    ```text
    Sell my 20 Yes shares at 0.60 or better.
    ```

    Or hold until the market resolves. A share on the losing side goes to zero.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every order needs your confirmation, and nothing here is financial advice.

## Going further: split and merge

Besides buying and selling, Hyperliquid lets you convert between USDC and shares. **Split** turns USDC into equal Yes and No shares; **merge** turns matching Yes and No shares back into USDC. For questions, **merge a question** and **negate** work across named outcomes. Most traders only buy and sell; these matter if you hold both sides or make markets.

```text
Split 50 USDC into Yes and No shares on that outcome. Show me what I will hold first.
```

## Check it worked

- Your share count is a whole number and matches the order.
- The market you hold is the one whose expiry and target you read in step 2.

## Common mistakes

- **Trading the display name.** Two markets called the same thing can have different targets and expiries.
- **Fractional sizes.** Orders must be in whole shares.
- **Looking for USDH.** Outcome markets use USDC.
- **Confusing a question with a standalone market.** In a question, only one named outcome can win.
- **Building orders by hand in your own code.** Outcome shares use a special asset id. Read the formula on the [Hyperliquid tool](/mcp/tools-hyperliquid) page.
