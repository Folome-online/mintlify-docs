Whether you open a sample or type your own question, everything on the result follows from one choice: the Polymarket market Alchemy decides your event is about. The clearer the event, the better that match. A good event takes ten seconds to write and saves a paid run.

## What Alchemy does with your words

1. It reads the event and states it back in the first step, for example "Understood: Fed cuts rates in December". If you named a focus, such as "impact on tech", it adds that too.
2. It searches Polymarket with several short queries built from your words.
3. It picks a headline market, favoring markets that share your names and key words, that are still live, and that have real volume.

So names, dates and a clear outcome are what steer it.

## Five rules

Each rule below follows from how that search works.

<Steps>
  <Step title="One outcome per run">
    Ask about one thing that either happens or does not. Two events in one question get one muddled market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a date">
    "Before year end", "in December", "in 2027". Polymarket markets resolve on a date, and a dated event matches a dated market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use names">
    People, companies, countries and tickers are the strongest signal for finding the market. "Nvidia" beats "a big chipmaker".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it measurable">
    Prefer outcomes someone could settle: a rate cut, an election win, a price level, an earnings beat. "Markets get scary" cannot resolve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a focus only if you want one">
    End with "impact on tech" or "impact on energy" to put that angle first in the trades. Leave it out to see the whole map.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good vs weak

Here is what the rules do to some real questions.

| Weak | Better | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| what about AI stocks | Nvidia beats on earnings | One company, one outcome |
| rates | Fed cuts rates in December | A decision with a date |
| crypto going up? | Bitcoin new all-time high before year end | A level and a deadline |
| who wins the election and what happens to oil | JD Vance wins the 2028 election | One question per run; ask about oil separately |
| the economy is bad | US recession in 2027 | Measurable and dated |
| war stuff | Iran vs USA war impact | Names the countries |
| hacks | Market impact due to increase in cyber hacks | Says what you want: the market impact |

<Tip>
If Alchemy shows "no market prices this yet", it still builds trades from related markets and instruments. Try rewording with a name or a date, or ask about a nearby event that does have a market.
</Tip>

## Questions that work less well

Some questions are better asked somewhere else, or split up first.

- **Open questions.** "What should I buy?" has no outcome to price. Use Chat for that.
- **Single-asset orders.** "Long ETH 3x" is a trade, not an event. Chat or the Hyperliquid tools place it directly.
- **Long lists.** "Fed, oil, gold and the election" will pick one and drop the rest.
- **Side questions you did not mean.** Wording such as "by more than 10 points" or "how many seats" points Alchemy at a bracket market rather than the main one. Use it only when that is what you want.

## Before you send

- Is it one outcome?
- Is there a date?
- Is there a name?
- Could someone settle it as Yes or No?

If all four are yes, send it.
